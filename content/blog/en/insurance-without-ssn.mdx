Many Hispanic families live with the belief that they cannot get insurance in the United States without a Social Security Number. This belief costs them dearly: they live unprotected, exposed to accidents, illnesses, or losses that can destroy years of hard work.

The reality is different. And it's worth understanding.

## What Is an ITIN and Why Does It Matter?

The ITIN (Individual Taxpayer Identification Number) is a tax identification number issued by the IRS for people who need to comply with tax obligations but don't qualify for an SSN. Many insurance companies in the United States accept the ITIN as a valid form of identification to open a policy.

If you have an ITIN, you have access to more options than you think.

## What Insurance Can You Get Without an SSN?

### Life Insurance

Life insurance is possibly the most accessible for people without an SSN. Many companies evaluate your medical history and ability to pay, not your immigration status. You can protect your family today. Subject to terms, conditions, and underwriting approval depending on the provider and state.

### Auto Insurance

In several states, it's possible to get auto insurance with an ITIN or even without an SSN, especially if you have a valid state driver's license. California, Illinois, New York, and other states allow you to get a driver's license regardless of immigration status. Subject to terms and conditions.

### Health Insurance

Depending on your state of residence and situation, you may access health plans through the Marketplace or state programs. A certified advisor can guide you without compromising your privacy.

### Pet and Business Insurance

For these types of insurance, requirements are generally more flexible. In most cases, a valid ID and a payment method are sufficient.
